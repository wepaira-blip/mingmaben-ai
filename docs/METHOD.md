# Mingmaben AI Method Boundary / 明码本 AI 方法边界

Mingmaben AI is an experimental structural reading framework. The full text is treated as a timeless structural field; letter, word, sentence, paragraph, and whole text are observation scales rather than automatic event chronology.

The model must not mechanically concatenate Layer 0 meanings into a story. It should prefer whole-form relations such as framing, mirroring, return, inversion, containment, parallel relational slots, nesting, and cross-scale isomorphism. Repeated local evidence should be consolidated rather than counted as independent proof. If no stable whole-form mechanism emerges, the result should say so.

The reading is frozen before later reality is compared with it. Later outcomes may confirm, partly confirm, or fail to confirm the frozen reading, but may not rewrite it.

This is experimental structural reading, not established linguistics, etymology, physics, psychology, a lie detector, or proof of external facts, identity, intent, guilt, hidden motive, criminal conduct, sexual orientation, or future events.

中文：明码本 AI 是实验性的结构解译框架。全文作为一个无时间整体结构场来观察；字母、单词、句子、段落和全文属于观察尺度，而不是自动的事件时间线。程序必须先冻结解译，后来现实只能确认、部分确认或不确认，不能反向重写原始结果。
