# Privacy / 隐私

Submitted text is sent to the configured AI service for analysis. Frozen readings may be stored for repeatability and to preserve the first canonical result. Submitted text is not automatically published.

Do not submit passwords, bank details, government IDs, authentication secrets, or highly sensitive private material.

Feedback is associated with the Frozen ID. Payment details are not processed or stored by the Mingmaben AI application itself.

中文：提交文本会发送给配置的 AI 服务进行分析。为保证可重复性和首次冻结结果，系统可能保存冻结解译。提交文本不会自动公开。请勿提交密码、银行资料、政府证件号码、认证密钥或高度敏感私人信息。
