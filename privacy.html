Privacy Notice

Your submitted text is sent to the AI service to generate the requested analysis. Frozen readings may be stored to preserve experimental repeatability and the integrity of the first canonical result.

Your submitted text and frozen reading are not automatically published. A future public case library, if introduced, will require a separate explicit opt-in.

Please do not submit passwords, bank details, government IDs, authentication secrets, or other highly sensitive private material.

If you use your own OpenAI API Key, it is sent through the Mingmaben AI Worker only for that analysis request, is not stored by Mingmaben AI, and the model usage is charged to your own OpenAI API account. Do not reuse a key you are unwilling to revoke.

Feedback is linked to the Frozen ID. The public application does not store payment-card data or expose the developer's banking credentials.
